SUNSPEAR TURNAROUND (accepted second attempt)
Create an orthographic 3D creature modeling turnaround, four animals in one row: FRONT, LEFT side facing left, BACK, RIGHT side facing right. No lettering. Flat solid pale gray background. Original bronze fire dragon SUNSPEAR with four legs and two wings, wedge snout, lean body, long straight tail, copper throat, open jaw teeth visible. Critical wing design: EACH WING IS ONE SINGLE HUGE TRIANGLE, like the triangular lateen sail of a sailing boat or a pterosaur delta wing. ONE pointed wingtip per wing, long straight leading edge and single almost straight trailing edge from tip to hip. NOT the conventional bat wing with several finger-tips along the edge: no scalloped edges, no multiple tips. Supporting spars run INSIDE the triangle and terminate on its straight trailing edge. Wing membrane warm golden amber, body dark bronze, no upright dorsal spikes. Wings spread fully sideways horizontal, shoulder elbow wrist on leading edge, four legs separate underneath. Head and long straight tail level, neutral model bind pose. Same anatomy and pose from every camera, entire wing tips feet and tail inside each panel with generous blank margins, no overlaps between animals. Front/back wings extend laterally at body height; side wings seen edge-on, NOT raised vertically for presentation. Realistic stylized textured game creature on a uniform background with no cast shadows. 3:1 panorama.

RIMEPLUME TURNAROUND
Use case: stylized-concept. Production orthographic turnaround of RIMEPLUME, the silver feathered frost dragon in the TOP MIDDLE panel of the reference board. Only that creature. Four separate views in ONE HORIZONTAL ROW ordered FRONT, LEFT PROFILE facing left, BACK, RIGHT PROFILE facing right. Plain uniform RGB 235,235,235 background, no text, labels, borders, ground shadows. Four entire creatures uncut, generous spacing and margins. Identical six-limbed anatomy and pose in all four: four slim clawed standing legs, TWO separate huge feather wings spread laterally straight and level like T-pose, head level, long tapered tail extended straight back, jaw visibly parted exposing separate mandible and teeth. Narrow slate-black toothed beak-like muzzle, small swept feather crown, pearl scaled body with layered silver feather shoulder/chest mantle, slate ankles and claws, long slim feather-tipped tail. Wings have three clear structural finger rays with broad overlapping rigid silver primary feather vanes, layered secondary feathers on arm; keep feathers large and countable, not thousands of tiny quills. Visible shoulder elbow wrist structure, feather fans grow from wings not forelegs. Feather tips sharply shaped but natural, no crystals, no fur, no turbine tail. Front/back show full wingspan; side views naturally foreshorten wings, do not rotate to display them. Consistent scale/materials, neutral diffuse lighting, crisp realistic stylized textured game sculpt. Panorama 3:1. Only four animals, no extra detail insets.
