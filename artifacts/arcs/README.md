# Arc and spacing charts (2026-11-04)

`docs/ANIMATION.md`, "Arcs and spacing: the neck in the beat and the legs in
the brake". Drawn by `tools/rig_probes/plot_trace.py` from `trace_probe`.

- `flap-neck-no-hold.jpg`: the studio flap without the head hold. Every
  neck joint peaks on the chest's frame: the neck is a stick.
- `flap-neck-first-hold.jpg`: the first hold (an exact two-bone solve). The
  speed spikes at neck1 are pops, twice a beat.
- `flap-neck-dls.jpg`: the damped least squares hold. Smooth, and the
  head peaks lower and later than the chest.
- `brake-before.jpg`, `brake-after.jpg`: the studio brake. Before, square
  speed edges (hard starts and stops) and flat holds; after, bells that
  peak hip first, talons last.
- `melee-before.jpg`: the studio melee (bite, claw, tail), the baseline for
  the melee work.
- `roster-brake-a.jpg`, `roster-brake-b.jpg`: every release species in the
  brake (frames 60 and 100, and from below the front), the flap, the head
  close, and a landing.
- `melee-after.jpg`: the same melee after the weight pass (`ANIMATION.md`,
  "Weight in the strike"). Speed curves are bells, about 60% of the old
  peaks. The tail tip is still jagged through the whip.
